This extension represents the date from which a certain document is allowed to exchange on the infrastructure. This date may be in the past or in the future depending on when it is allowed to share a document over the national XDS infrastructure. 

# Note on previous support
In version 1.0.0 of the XDS-metadata standard [insert link] the validFrom date was represented for each code in an excel document. The purpose of moving this information to the DocumentReference profile is to clearly govern when a document can be exchanged from and allow codes to be used in different settings without the risk of misinterpreting the date.
